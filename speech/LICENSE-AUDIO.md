# Dance announcement recordings

The MP3 files under `speech/` (the spoken dance names, heat numbers and
"next dance" clips, in a female and a male voice) were generated with
[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) text-to-speech on its free plan.

**They are not covered by the MIT license of the code.** Free-plan output may
only be used for non-commercial purposes, with credit to ElevenLabs:

> Voices: elevenlabs.io

Use them for non-commercial events only. For commercial use, generate your own
clips with `speech/generate_announcements.py` under an ElevenLabs plan that
includes a commercial license, or switch the announcements off.
