Icon artwork used by this app
=============================

None of the artwork below is under the app's own MIT licence (LICENSE); each
keeps the terms named here.

The app icon (icon.png, icon.ico), the dancers with the note, is the icon
"Swing" by Icons8:
https://icons8.com/icons/set/swing
Used under the Icons8 free licence, which asks for a link to Icons8:
https://icons8.com — terms: https://icons8.com/license
Here it is drawn black on a white rounded tile.

The icons in the app's toolbars and panels are mostly Bootstrap Icons (MIT),
full text below.

The "bull" icon (Paso Doble) is derived from Twemoji's ox, U+1F402:
Copyright 2020 Twitter, Inc and other contributors
Graphics licensed under CC-BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
It has been flattened to a single colour for this app.
